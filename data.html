Capturing Monetary Sovereignty

Data from a study of consultation and legislative materials on stablecoin remuneration restrictions in the European Union, United Kingdom, and United States. The package accompanies Capturing Monetary Sovereignty: On the prohibition of stablecoin remuneration.

It contains the source register and links, coded observations, codebook, review records, and scripts reproducing the paper’s counts and rationale heatmap. The substantive source cutoff is 2 October 2026; later qualitative checks are documented separately.
